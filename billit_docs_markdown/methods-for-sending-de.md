# Methods for sending DE

In Germany, e-invoicing must be according to the European standard EN16931. E-invoice delivery to customers can be done via 2 transport methods : E-mail and Peppol.

Below a summary per method:

| Transport Type | Current Adoption Rate | What is needed | Associated file format |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Email | Preferred by most receivers | Bill-to E-mail address of customer | Most common is ZUGFeRD, alternative can be XRechnung |
| Peppol | Accepted by a minority | Peppol registration info of the customer if existing | Determined by Billit based on Peppol registration |
