## What is Mandatory

When / for which type of enterprise the PA invoicing is mandatory:

| Size of company | Invoice Sending | Invoice Receiving | E-reporting B2Bi - B2C - Flux10 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Large Companies / Medium sized companies | 01/09/2026 | 01/09/2026 | 01/09/2026 |
| Companies smaller than medium | 01/09/2027 | 01/09/2026 | 01/09/2027 |

Medium company = ETI = Entreprise de Taille Intermédiaire (Intermediate-Sized Enterprise):

- Between 250 and 4.999 employees,
- and:
  - Annual turnover ≤ €1.5 billion
  - or Total balance sheet ≤ €2 billion

For companies smaller than medium, the sending can be initiated on the date of obligation (01/09/2027) or earlier, e.g. in the first half of 2027. The last option would allow to start with a few customers and to gather experience.
