## Developer Onboarding Flow Is Useful and Required, why ?

- [x]  Billit support can has access to the status of the developer onboarding, giving rapid insight.
- [x]  Via a wizard you respond to questions, this gives a clear insight in what to do and where to access the correct information.
- [x]  Good understanding what has been done, whether you are ready to move to production.
