# Approval Process

Great job! You've reached one of the most crucial pages.

You've built your integration and want it to be published on the Billit integration page or go public with it. ?

- Yes: Just ensure you've completed everything listed below, and you'll be ready to proceed!
- No: You can still get a demo review with feedback, no formal approval is needed.

# Billit Integration Review Flow

**Is the integration working as expected in the Sandbox?**

- ✅ Perfect! Please schedule a demo meeting with one of our API engineers. [How do I request a demo for my integration?](how-do-i-request-a-demo-for-my-integration.md)

- 🚩 If the integration is not working as expected, continue refining it. The API Engineer will not approve production access if the integration is not functioning correctly. If you need help or are unsure about using our API, visit [What support can I get as a developer?](https://docs.billit.be/docs/what-support-can-i-get-as-a-developer)

**Next step: Moving the integration to production.**

- ✅ Once successful and tested, email to your technical contact person, or if unknown at [appreview@billit.be](mailto:appreview@billit.be). They will confirm whether an appreview is needed or not.

- 🚩 If the production swap was unsuccessful and you cannot determine the cause, please contact support with as much documentation as possible.

That's it! Once you've completed the steps above, you're ready. Great job, and thank you for building your awesome integration.
