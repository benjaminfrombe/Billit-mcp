## Basic Steps

Go to the MyBillit interface and manually create the Account on Billit. This applies to the Sandbox and/or Production Environment.

Data to be filled in on the My company part:

![](https://files.readme.io/474298384aceb4e7c99fc54904a4a2a4fe1f3e2c2ee130f75d1c227185501bfd-2025-10-28_09-21-36a.png)

The green boxes are mandatory or highly recommended information.

1: The email adress added here will trigger and email for verification. Click on the link in the email, it will bring you to the MyBillit site for login. Afterwards, this email address is verified. It can be changed later. The email address can be an official email address of the company, this can be a group mailbox or a mailbox of a person.

Check status on dashboard

On MyBillit Interface, in Dashboard menu, check completeness of the account.

Complete Company Profile and User Profile if Needed.

## Check and Complete Company Profile

![](https://files.readme.io/8a2c0603c59fc5bc37fdf08d77f5b98f2dcc782bb27d21a875f0e783504766e8-2025-10-28_09-30-53.png)

When you click on Phone number or Iban number, and the information is missing, then you are able to fill in the missing information. About the information:

| Information | Action | Sandbox | Production |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IBAN | Fill in, check | Fill in, IBAN verification optional | Mandatory |
| Phone Number | Fill in, check | Fill in, verify | Mandatory |
